# Customer email policy note (shipping-confirmation email)

For the engineer wiring `CUSTOMER_EMAIL_POLICY_NOTE`. Paste the paragraph below as the
env value (plain text, one line). Only facts that are decided are stated; the two
`[TO CONFIRM: …]` markers must be replaced by the owner's final wording **or removed
together with their sentence** before any real customer email is sent. Do not send this
text to customers while a marker is still in it.

## Paragraph (English, for the env var)

```text
Returns and refunds: our return window is [TO CONFIRM: return window] and the steps are on our Returns & Refunds page (returns.html on the store); to start a return, email services@apgo.com.tw with your order number. Delivery time: [TO CONFIRM: delivery time]. Questions about your order? Email services@apgo.com.tw or call +886 2 2903 0101, Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00 Taiwan time (UTC+8).
```

Notes for the engineer:

- Keep it one paragraph, plain text. The store pages and address are in `prototype/returns.html` / `contact.html`; use the absolute store URL of the returns page if the email template wants a link.
- Support hours are Taiwan time (UTC+8); do not convert them in the email.

## Still TO CONFIRM (owner / counsel decisions)

These are marked on the pages with a yellow `[TO CONFIRM: …]` highlight
(`<mark data-to-confirm>`, generated by `scripts/build-policy-pages.py`).

Returns & Refunds (`returns.html`):

- Return window (number of days, counted from delivery or not)
- Which products are accepted back (unopened/unused only? opened/used?)
- Reporting window for damaged / defective / wrong items
- Remedy for damaged items (replacement, refund or both; original and return shipping covered?)
- Who pays return shipping for change-of-mind returns
- What a refund includes (product price and/or shipping fees)
- Refund processing time
- Order cancellation rules (e.g. until it ships)

Terms of Sale (`terms.html`):

- Shipping fees and any free-shipping terms
- Whether, where and at what rate sales tax is collected
- Shipping regions (US addresses is only the plan)
- Shipping methods and delivery time for each
- When risk of loss passes to the buyer (counsel)
- Liability cap (counsel)
- Governing law and jurisdiction (counsel)

Privacy Policy (`privacy.html`):

- Which fulfillment partner(s) receive order data
- Record retention period

Not on the policy pages, but also undecided and shown by the store UI from the Worker's
placeholder pricing (`worker/pricing.js`): product prices, the Standard / Express shipping
labels, delivery-day text and fees on the checkout shipping step. These are gated by
`PRICING_APPROVED` in production.
