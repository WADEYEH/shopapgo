**Eyebrow**

MORE THAN A CLEAN CAR

**H2**

A better finish starts where the wash ends.

**Intro**

A wash removes the grime. The finishing step gives clean paint deeper
reflections, tighter water beading, and the just-detailed look that lasts beyond
wash day.

**Benefit 1**

WATER BEADS, NOT SHEETS

A hydrophobic finish helps water gather into tight beads and move off the
surface.

GLOSS YOU CAN SEE

Bring out a deeper, more reflective shine without polishing equipment.

**Benefit 3**

ONE FINISH FOR THE EXTERIOR

Care for compatible paint, glass, trim, wheels, lights, and wraps as directed.

### D204 tab

**Tab label**

D204 · Apply after drying

**Video title**

How to apply APGO D204

**Steps**

1. Wash the vehicle and dry it completely.
2. Work on a cool surface away from direct sun.
3. Spray and spread evenly, one section at a time.
4. Buff with a clean, dry microfiber towel.

### D215 tab

**Tab label**

D215 · Apply while wet

**Video title**

How to apply APGO D215

**Steps**

1. Wash and rinse the vehicle.
2. Leave the clean paint wet.
3. Spray and spread over one section.
4. Dry and finish with the recommended cloth until the surface is clear and
   even.

Follow the current product label for surface compatibility and application
directions.
