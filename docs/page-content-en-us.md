## How to apply

Eyebrow: `APPLICATION`

H2:

> USE THE METHOD DESIGNED FOR EACH PRODUCT.

### D204 · Dry-surface application

1. Wash the vehicle.
2. Fully dry the paint.
3. Work on cool paint in the shade.
4. Spray and spread one small section at a time.
5. Buff with a separate clean microfiber towel.

### D215 · Wet-surface application

1. Wash the vehicle.
2. Keep the paint wet for application.
3. Spray and spread with a clean wet cloth.
4. Complete the final drying step with a clean towel.

## FAQ

### What is the main difference between D204 and D215?

D204 is a 300 mL dry-surface application used after clean automotive paint is
fully dry. D215 is a 200 mL wet-surface application used while clean automotive
paint is still wet after washing, before the final drying step.

### Can I use the same application method for both?

No. D204 and D215 are designed for different points in the wash routine. Read
and follow the current label for the specific product you are using.

### Which one should I choose?

Choose by application routine. Select D204 if you prefer a separate spray,
spread, and buff step after drying. Select D215 if you prefer to apply while the
paint is still wet before completing the final drying step.

### Can I use these products on glass, trim, wheels, PPF, or wraps?

Use each product only on surfaces identified by its current label. Do not assume
that every automotive material or finish is compatible. Contact APGO before use
when the surface is not identified on the label.

### Where are price, shipping, availability, and returns handled?

on Amazon.com.

### How can I contact APGO about product use?
