05 / Before you begin

Four useful answers.

01Should I choose the dry or wet application?+

Choose D204 if you are willing to dry the paint first and want deeper color, stronger gloss and the longest protection. Choose D215 if you want to apply directly after the final rinse and reduce towel drag while you dry.

02What should I do before application?+

Thoroughly wash and rinse the vehicle first. For D204, fully dry the paint before application. For D215, keep the freshly rinsed paint wet. Wet application can reduce dry towel drag, but never wipe over sand, grit or remaining dirt.

03When should I reapply each coating?+

D204 provides protection for up to 180 days and 30+ washes. D215 provides protection for up to 120 days and 20+ washes. Reapply when you reach the interval or when you want to refresh the finish and water behavior.

04How many full-car applications are in each bottle?+

Both products provide approximately 8–10 complete full-car applications per bottle when applied in a thin, even layer.
