## Owner decisions and shipping research

Read [launch decisions](launch-decisions-2026-10-04.md) and [MCF/retail shipping research](mcf-shipping-review-2026-10-04.md), including its final US ZIP follow-up.

- Website collects through Airwallex; Amazon fulfills through MCF.
- Initial products are single-bottle D204 and D215, using Amazon seller SKUs `D204` and `D215`. Multiple bottles per cart are not prohibited; cloth kits and virtual bundles are excluded initially.
- Intended base prices match the observed Amazon offers: D204 USD 59.99, D215 USD 29.99. They have **not** been applied. Checkout still has test defaults D204 USD 29.90 / D215 USD 24.90, Standard free / Express USD 9, and undecided zero tax.
- Owner wants customer shipping rules to align with Amazon's retail experience. Passing MCF cost directly to the customer is a superseded proposal.
- At five sampled retail ZIPs (Seattle, New York, Honolulu, Anchorage, San Juan), D204 single-bottle offers showed free standard shipping; D215 showed free standard shipping on eligible Amazon-shipped orders over USD 35. Faster dates and Prime benefits varied. This is product-page evidence, not checkout fees or MCF eligibility.
- Exact below-threshold and paid expedited retail fees remain unverified: Amazon blocks this signed-in seller from adding their own product to the cart. No workaround or purchase was attempted. USD 35 website free shipping was recommended, but no final website rule was approved or configured.
- FBA listing fees, seller-fulfilled shipping templates, retail customer fees and MCF charges are distinct. Do not copy the default seller-fulfilled template into checkout or promise Prime next-day service through ordinary MCF.
- Live MCF samples: D204 single Standard USD 9.24 / Expedited USD 13.58; D215 single USD 8.91 / USD 12.22 at Seattle/New York. Two-bottle quotes differ from twice the single price. These are time-specific costs charged to APGO, not approved customer freight.
- Owner said US mainland; exact boundaries/address types are not finalized. Retail offers to Alaska/Hawaii/Puerto Rico do not establish low MCF cost or universal coverage.
- Tax/collecting entity and customer return rules remain open; zero tax and free returns are not approved.
- Team order/exception alert recipient is `wadeyeh@apgo.com.tw`; production configuration has not been set.
- Owner declined a real fulfillment test. Do not create/hold MCF orders, enable auto-fulfillment or use fake sandbox orders for live shipment. Genuine end-to-end shipping remains unverified.
