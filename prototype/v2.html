04 / The real process

Watch the finish happen.

No guesswork. Choose a routine, then watch the actual hand application from first spray to final towel.

Start with the moment that fits your wash.

D204 / dry application

A deliberate final pass.

Wash and fully dry. Work one cool section at a time, then buff the product clear to reveal the finish.

  1. 01
    Start clean and fully dry.

    Move the car out of direct sun and let the paint cool before application.

  2. 02
    Spray and spread one section.

    Build a thin, even layer with a clean microfiber towel.

  3. 03
    Buff to a clear gloss.

    Turn to a clean dry side and finish the section before moving on.

D215 / wet application

The finish inside the dry.

Keep clean paint wet after washing. Apply and spread D215, then towel-dry to finish both steps together.

  1. 01
    Wash away the reset.

    Start with clean paint and rinse away the remaining wash solution.

  2. 02
    Keep the paint wet.

    D215 goes on before the final towel-dry, not after it.

  3. 03
    Spray and spread.

    Distribute the product across the wet section with a clean cloth.

  4. 04
    Towel-dry to finish.

    Remove the water and complete the finish in the same final pass.
