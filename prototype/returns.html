Our promise

If something is wrong with your order, we will make it right. This page covers orders placed on this store. For Amazon orders, use Your Orders on Amazon.

Returns

  • You may return an unopened, unused product within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the product price.
  • Opened or used products can be returned only if they are defective or were not what you ordered.
  • To start a return, email services@apgo.com.tw with your order number and the reason. We will reply with return instructions. Please do not ship anything back before you hear from us.

Damaged, defective or wrong item

Email services@apgo.com.tw within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the item and packaging. We will send a replacement or refund you in full, including the original shipping you paid, and cover the return shipping if a return is needed.

Return shipping

For other returns, you pay the cost of shipping the item back.

How refunds work

After we receive and check the returned item, we refund the product price to the original payment method through Airwallex. Banks usually post the refund within 5–10 business days. Express shipping fees are not refunded unless the return is because of our error.

Order changes and cancellations

We can cancel an order that has not shipped yet. Email services@apgo.com.tw as soon as possible with your order number.

Questions
