What is the main difference between D204 and D215?

D204 is a 300 mL dry-application product used after automotive paint is clean and fully dry. D215 is a 200 mL wet-application product used while clean paint is still wet after washing.

Can I use the same application method for both?

No. D204 follows a spray, spread, and buff routine on dry paint. D215 is applied and spread while the paint is wet, before the final drying step. Always follow the current product label.

Which product should I choose?

Choose based on where you want application to fit. Pick D204 for a separate step after drying, or D215 for application during the wash-and-dry routine while paint is still wet.

Can I use these products on glass, trim, wheels, PPF, or wraps?

This page is organized around application on automotive paint. Check the current label on your bottle for approved surfaces, or contact APGO support before applying to another material.
